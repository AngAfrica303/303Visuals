About 303 VISUALS

Photography built around stories, energy, and detail.

303 VISUALS is a photography brand based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, created to capture meaningful experiences with a strong visual identity. We work across event coverage, sports photography, portrait storytelling, and fashion imagery with a sharp eye for mood, movement, and composition.

Whether working with brands, teams, individuals, or event organizers, we bring a focused, professional, and creative approach that helps every frame stand out. We are also available to travel across Tanzania and internationally for projects that need a fresh visual perspective.

  • Professional coverage for live events and campaigns
  • Strong sports and action storytelling
  • Clean portrait and fashion direction
  • Fast delivery and client-focused communication
